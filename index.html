The problem

Scammers can copy everything except the real phone

Fake boss on WhatsApp

A new number with the owner’s photo asks Accounts for an urgent, secret payment.

“I’m in a meeting, can’t talk. Pay this vendor now.”

Cloned voices

A few seconds of audio is enough to clone a voice and call a parent or an employee.

“Mummy, it’s me. I’m in trouble, send money.”

Deepfake video calls

A familiar face on a video call pushes staff to approve transfers or new bank details.

“Approve the new account details. Auditors are waiting.”
How it works

A second check, from the one device a scammer doesn’t have

A request arrives

On WhatsApp, a call, email or video, claiming to be from your boss, colleague or family member.

You tap Verify

Pick who it claims to be from and describe what they asked for. Verth sends a check to their own signed-in phone.

They answer Yes or No

You see the answer in seconds. No answer within 3 minutes means don’t act.

On a live call? Ask for their Verth code

Every person’s app shows a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds, like the codes in Google Authenticator. Ask the caller to read it out and check it in Verth. A deepfake can copy a face and a voice, but not a code on someone else’s phone.

Guidelines

When to use Verth

Use a check whenever a message asks you to move money, change where money goes, or hand over access, especially when it’s urgent or secret.

  • Before sending money or paying an invoice someone asked for
  • Before changing bank details for a vendor, salary or rent
  • Before sharing an OTP, password or reset link
  • When a family member says they’re in an emergency
  • When someone asks you to keep a request secret
  • Before sharing confidential files or customer data

Questions

Good to know

Does Verth read my WhatsApp, calls or email?

No. Verth never connects to your messages. You tell it what came in, and it asks the real person.

Does the other person need Verth too?

Yes. Checks go only to people in your circle, on the phone or laptop where they’re signed in. That’s what makes the answer trustworthy.

What if they don’t answer?

A check expires after 3 minutes. No answer means don’t act. Try again or reach them another way you already trust.

Who can join my circle?

Anyone you give the invite code to, so share it only with people you trust. You can see everyone in the circle at any time.

Is my data safe?

Accounts use Google’s Firebase with email confirmation. Security rules make sure only people in your circle can see its checks, and only the named person can answer a check.
