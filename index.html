New · Scam check

Got a weird SMS, email or call? Paste it, or send a screenshot.

Verth reads the message, link or phone number and tells you exactly which red flags it found, in plain words. It knows India’s common scams: fake KYC updates, “digital arrest” calls, parcel and electricity threats, task jobs, and QR codes that take money instead of sending it. Not good with copy and paste? Just upload a screenshot or photo of the message. Verth reads it on your phone, and the picture is never uploaded.

SMS · VM-SBIYNO · 10:42Dear Customer, your SBI YONO account will be blocked today. Update your PAN KYC immediately: http://sbi-yono-kyc.xyz/update

High risk: this looks like a scam

  • Suspicious link: sbi-yono-kyc.xyzPretends to be State Bank of India. Cheap “.xyz” web address. Not a secure (https) link.
  • KYC, PAN or Aadhaar “update”Banks don’t ask you to update KYC through a link. Use the official app or branch.
  • Threatens to block you, and rushes youPressure like “blocked today” is designed to stop you thinking.

How it works

A second check, from the one device a scammer doesn’t have

1

A request arrives

On WhatsApp, a call, email or video, claiming to be from your boss, colleague or family member.

2

You tap Verify

Pick who it claims to be from. Verth asks them on their own registered phone, not the number that contacted you.

3

They answer, signed

Their Yes or No is signed by their device, so a stolen password can’t fake it. No answer in 3 minutes means don’t act.

On a live call? Ask for their Verth code

Each pair of people shares a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds, like Google Authenticator. A deepfake can copy a face and a voice, but not a code on someone else’s phone.

On your phone

Put Verth on your home screen

Install Verth like an app. On Android you can then share any SMS, WhatsApp message, email or link straight to Verth from the app you’re in.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open umeshdk22.github.io/verth in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Verth below, or the ⋮ menu → Install app.
  3. In WhatsApp, Messages or Gmail, long-press a message or screenshot → Share → Verth. It opens ready to check.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open umeshdk22.github.io/verth in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. To check something, copy it and paste it into Verth’s Scam check.

iPhone doesn’t let websites appear in the share menu yet.

Guidelines

When to use Verth

Check before you move money, change where money goes, or hand over access, especially when someone is rushing you or asking you to keep it secret.

  • Before sending money or paying an invoice someone asked for
  • Before changing bank details for a vendor, salary or rent
  • Before sharing an OTP, password or reset link
  • When a family member says they’re in an emergency
  • When an SMS, email or link threatens, rushes or rewards you
  • When an unknown number claims to be a bank, police or courier

Questions

Good to know

Does Verth read my WhatsApp, SMS, calls or email?

No. Verth never connects to your messages. You paste what you want checked, and the check runs on your own device.

Can Scam check be wrong?

Yes. It explains red flags it can see, but no tool catches every scam, and “no red flags” doesn’t prove something is safe. If a message asks for money, codes or personal details, check with the real person or company first.

Can Verth tell me if a job offer is real?

It checks the warning signs: whether the email really came from the company’s own domain, look-alike websites, requests for money, WhatsApp interviews and requests for documents. It can’t see inside a company, so always confirm the job on the company’s official Careers page too.

Does the other person need Verth too?

For verification checks, yes: they go only to people in your circle, on the phone where they’re signed in. Scam check works on its own.

What if they don’t answer?

A check expires after 3 minutes. No answer means don’t act. Reach them another way you already trust.

Is my data safe?

Answers are signed by a key that never leaves the person’s phone, new members need admin approval, and the log can’t be edited or deleted. Scam reports store only a scrambled fingerprint, never what you pasted. Read the full security notes.

I’ve already lost money. What now?

Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) or report at cybercrime.gov.in straight away, and tell your bank. The faster you report, the better the chance of stopping the money.
